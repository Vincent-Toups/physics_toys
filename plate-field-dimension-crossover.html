Reading the model. The plot and vector sketch use a smooth crossover model, E(r) = 1 / [r(r + ℓ)], with effective crossover length ℓ = 4/d. At distances small compared with ℓ, it behaves like 1/r; far beyond ℓ, it behaves like 1/r². Bringing the plates together increases ℓ, so the fitted exponent over the plotted range moves from 2 toward 1. This is a pedagogical confinement model; the sketch does not solve the full boundary-value problem for neutral infinite conductors.
